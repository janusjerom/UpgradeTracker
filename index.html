Put it on your home screen

On your Realme (Realme UI 4.0, Android 13), open this page's link in Chrome — not inside the Muse app — then:

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in Chrome's top-right corner.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm — it lands on your home screen as CoC Tracker with its own hammer icon, and opens full-screen like a widget.

Your accounts are saved in Chrome on this phone only, so use the same browser each time. If it ever opens as “Muse” instead, you opened the Muse-app copy — use the link in Chrome and the tracker icon will be separate.
